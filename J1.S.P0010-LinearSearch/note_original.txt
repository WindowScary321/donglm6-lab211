* Giai thich thuat toan :
thuat toan linear search la thuat toan tim kiem tuyen tinh
phan mem duyet qua tung phan tu trong mang tu dau den cuoi
So sanh tung phan tu trong mang voi so can tim
Neu tim thay thi se luu lai vi tri cua phan tu do
Neu so can tim xuat hien nhieu lan, tiep tuc duyet den het mang de lay tat ca cac vi tri
Neu duyet het mang ma khong tim thay thi thong bao khong ton tai trong mang

* Yeu cau de bai:
Nguoi dung nhap so luong phan tu cua mang la mot so nguyen duong n
Phan mem tao ra mang chua cac so nguyen ngau nhien tu -n den n
In mang da sinh ra
Nguoi dung nhap so can tim trong mang
Phan mem dung thuat toan linear search de tim index cua so can tim trong mang
In index cua so can tim, neu so can tim xuat hien nhieu lan thi in ra index cua tat ca cac vi tri
Neu khong tim thay thi bao khong ton tai trong mang
