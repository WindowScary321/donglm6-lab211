* Yeu cau phan mem
Chuong trinh quan ly sinh vien bang Java Console, ap dung lap trinh huong doi tuong (OOP)
Su dung ArrayList de luu tru danh sach sinh vien, chi su dung cac lop va chuc nang cua Java Core
Hien thi menu chinh:
1. Create
2. Find and Sort
3. Update/Delete
4. Report
5. Exit

Moi ban ghi gom cac thong tin: ID, Student Name, Semester va Course Name
Chi chap nhan 3 mon hoc: Java, .Net, C/C++
Chuc nang Create cho phep nhap thong tin tu ban phim va tao it nhat 5 sinh vien
Khi da nhap du 5 ban ghi, hoi "Do you want to continue (Y/N)?", chon Y de nhap tiep, chon N de quay lai menu chinh
Sau moi ban ghi them thanh cong tu moc nay tro di, tiep tuc hoi Y/N
Chuc nang Find and Sort tim theo ten hoac mot phan ten, sap xep ket qua theo Student Name tang dan va hien thi Student Name, Semester, Course Name
Chuc nang Update/Delete tim theo ID, cho phep chon U de cap nhat hoac D de xoa
Chuc nang Report thong ke so lan hoc tung mon cua moi sinh vien, hien thi Student Name | Course | Total 
(sap xep theo Student Name tang dan, khong phan biet hoa thuong. Neu trung ten thi giu thu tu xuat hien ban dau)
Chon Exit de ket thuc chuong trinh

Cac quy uoc:
- Mot sinh vien hoc mot mon trong mot hoc ky
- Khong cho phep hai thong tin co cung ID, Semester va Course Name
- Mot sinh vien duoc hoc nhieu mon khac nhau trong cung hoc ky, hoac hoc lai mot mon o hoc ky khac
- Mot sinh vien khong duoc dang ky cung mot mon nhieu lan trong cung hoc ky
  Thong tin bi xem la trung khi: ID + Semester + Course Name giong voi thong tin truoc
- ID, Student Name va Semester khong duoc de trong hoac chi gom khoang trang
- ID bat dau bang S hoac s, theo sau la it nhat mot chu so
- Cac thong tin cung ID phai su dung cung Student Name, khi nhap ID da ton tai thi tu dong lay ten da co
- Neu sinh vien dang ky nhieu mon trong 1 hoac nhieu ky, hien thi danh sach de nguoi dung chon dung ban ghi can cap nhat hoac xoa

* Mo ta thuat toan
Chuong trinh gom lop Student luu thong tin, lop quan ly xu ly danh sach va lop Main hien thi menu, nhan lua chon
Lop Student gom cac thuoc tinh id, studentName, semester va courseName
Su dung ArrayList<Student> de them, tim kiem, cap nhat va xoa ban ghi

1. Hien thi menu:
   Hien thi 5 lua chon Create, Find and Sort, Update/Delete, Report va Exit
   Doc lua chon tu ban phim, chuyen sang so nguyen va kiem tra trong khoang 1 den 5
   Neu rong, ko phai so nguyen hoac ngoai khoang thi thong bao loi
   Neu chon 1 den 4 thi goi chuc nang tuong ung, thuc hien xong quay lai menu
   Neu chon 5 thi ket thuc chuong trinh

2. Tao sinh vien:
   Nhap ID, kiem tra dung dang S/s theo sau la it nhat mot chu so
   Neu rong hoac sai dinh dang thi thong bao loi va nhap lai
   Tim ID trong danh sach, neu da ton tai thi lay Student Name cua ID do, neu chua ton tai thi nhap ten moi
   Nhap Semester va Course Name, kiem tra du lieu khong rong va mon hoc thuoc Java, .Net, C/C++
   Duyet danh sach de kiem tra 3 thong tin ID, Semester, Course Name
   Neu da ton tai thi thong bao trung lap va yeu cau nhap lai
   Neu khong trung lap thi tao doi tuong Student va them vao ArrayList
   Khi danh sach co tu 5 tro len, hoi co muon tiep tuc hay khong
   Neu nhap Y thi nhap sinh vien tiep theo, neu nhap N thi quay lai menu
   Neu nhap lua chon khac Y/N thi thong bao loi va hoi lai
   Neu vao lai Create khi danh sach da du 5 thi khong bat nhap them 5 ban ghi moi, sau khi them mot ban ghi, hoi Y/N

3. Tim kiem va sap xep:
   Neu danh sach rong thi thong bao khong co sinh vien de tim
   Nhap ten hoac mot phan ten, kiem tra khong de trong
   Chuyen ten va tu khoa ve cung dang chu thuong, dung contains de tim tat ca ban ghi co ten chua tu khoa
   Them cac ban ghi phu hop vao danh sach ket qua
   Neu ket qua rong thi thong bao khong tim thay sinh vien
   Neu co ket qua thi dung Collections.sort() voi Comparator<Student>, cai dat compare() de so sanh Student Name khong phan biet hoa thuong
   Sap xep theo toan bo Student Name "va id" tang dan (giang vien yeu cau sap xep theo ten va id, phong truong hop sinh vien trung ten)
   Hien thi Student Name, Semester va Course Name cua tat ca ban ghi tim duoc
   Chi sap xep danh sach ket qua, khong can thay doi thu tu danh sach goc

4. Cap nhat hoac xoa:
   Neu danh sach rong thi thong bao khong co du lieu de cap nhat hoac xoa
   Nhap ID can tim, kiem tra dung dang S/s va chu so; neu sai thi yeu cau nhap lai ID
   Khi ID hop le, lay tat ca ban ghi co ID tuong ung
   Neu khong tim thay thi thong bao ID khong ton tai va quay lai menu
   Neu tim thay nhieu ban ghi thi hien thi kem so thu tu va cho nguoi dung chon mot ban ghi (them sap xep vao day)
   Kiem tra so thu tu la so nguyen trong khoang 1 den so ban ghi tim duoc
   Hoi "Do you want to update (U) or delete (D) student?"
   Chi chap nhan U/u hoac D/d; neu nhap sai thi yeu cau nhap lai

   - Neu chon U:
     Nhap thong tin moi, kiem tra du lieu giong khi tao sinh vien, ke ca quy uoc ID bat dau bang S/s va theo sau la chu so
     Neu nhap ID da ton tai thi su dung ten cua ID do de giu thong tin nhat quan
     Neu nhap ID moi thi yeu cau nhap Student Name moi
     Kiem tra bo ba ID, Semester, Course Name voi cac ban ghi khac, bo qua chinh ban ghi dang sua
     Neu trung voi ban ghi khac thi thong bao loi va giu nguyen du lieu cu
     Neu thong tin hop le thi cap nhat dung ban ghi da chon va thong bao thanh cong
     Neu khong thay doi thong tin thi khong xem ban ghi la trung voi chinh no

   - Neu chon D:
     Xoa dung doi tuong Student da chon khoi ArrayList
     Khong xoa tat ca cac ban ghi cung ID neu nguoi dung chi chon mot ban ghi
     Thong bao xoa thanh cong va quay lai menu

5. Bao cao:
   Neu danh sach rong thi thong bao khong co du lieu de bao cao
   Duyet tung ban ghi va nhom theo ID va Course Name
   Voi moi nhom, dem so ban ghi cua sinh vien hoc mon do qua cac hoc ky
   Co the dung mot Map voi khoa gom ID va Course Name, moi lan gap nhom do thi tang Total len 1
   Sap xep danh sach bao cao theo Student Name tang dan, khong phan biet hoa thuong
   Dung Collections.sort() voi Comparator<Report>, so sanh ten bang compareToIgnoreCase()
   Neu trung ten thi giu thu tu xuat hien ban dau cua cac nhom
   Hien thi Student Name | Course | Total
   Khong tach nhom theo Semester vi bao cao can tong so lan hoc mon do
   Khong gop hai sinh vien khac ID chi vi trung Student Name
   Vi du: S1 hoc Java o Fall2024 va Spring2025 thi hien thi Nguyen Van A | Java | 2
   Neu S1 con hoc .Net thi hien thi them mot dong Nguyen Van A | .Net | 1
   Moi nhom chi hien thi mot dong, khong lap lai dong bao cao cho tung ban ghi
   Bao cao duoc tinh tu du lieu hien tai de phan anh dung ket qua sau khi them, sua hoac xoa
