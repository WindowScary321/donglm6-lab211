* Yeu cau phan mem
Chuong trinh mo phong chuc nang dang nhap cua TBank
Hien thi menu gom 3 lua chon: 1. Vietnamese, 2. English, 3. Exit
Nguoi dung chon 1 de su dung giao dien tieng Viet, chon 2 de su dung giao dien tieng Anh, chon 3 de thoat chuong trinh
Sau khi chon ngon ngu, nguoi dung lan luot nhap so tai khoan, mat khau va captcha
So tai khoan phai gom dung 10 chu so
Mat khau phai co tu 8 den 31 ky tu, gom chu cai va chu so (phai co it nhat mot chu cai va mot chu so)
Chuong trinh sinh ngau nhien mot ma captcha gom 5 ky tu va hien thi de nguoi dung nhap
Nguoi dung nhap mot ky tu co trong ma captcha
Cac loi nhap lieu va loi nhac phai hien thi theo ngon ngu da chon; neu nhap sai thi thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
Khi so tai khoan, mat khau va captcha deu hop le thi thong bao dang nhap thanh cong

* Mo ta thuat toan
Lop Main hien thi menu, dieu huong theo lua chon cua nguoi dung va thuc hien dang nhap
Lop Ebank gom cac ham doi ngon ngu, kiem tra so tai khoan, kiem tra mat khau, sinh captcha va kiem tra captcha

1. Chon ngon ngu:
   Kiem tra lua chon menu, chi chap nhan cac gia tri 1, 2, 3; neu sai thi thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
   Neu chon 3 thi thoat chuong trinh
   Neu chon 1 hoac 2, goi setLocale(Locale locale) de chon ngon ngu tuong ung
   Dung ResourceBundle doc noi dung theo key trong hai file Language_en_US.properties va Language_vi_VN.properties de lay loi nhac va thong bao loi

2. Kiem tra so tai khoan:
   Ham checkaccountNumber(String accountNumber) dung Regex de kiem tra chuoi nhap vao co dung 10 chu so hay khong
   Giu so tai khoan o dang String de khong lam mat chu so 0 o dau
   Neu khong hop le thi tra ve false, ham login hien thi thong bao loi theo ngon ngu da chon va yeu cau nhap lai so tai khoan
   Neu hop le thi cho phep nhap mat khau

3. Kiem tra mat khau:
   Ham checkPassword(String password) dung Regex de kiem tra do dai tu 8 den 31 ky tu va dieu kien gom ca chu cai lan chu so
   Khong chap nhan mat khau chi co chu, chi co so, co khoang trang
   Neu khong hop le thi tra ve false, ham login hien thi thong bao loi theo ngon ngu da chon va yeu cau nhap lai
   Neu hop le thi tra ve true va cho phep dien captcha

4. Sinh ma captcha:
   Ham generateCaptcha() dung Random sinh ma captcha co 5 ky tu gom cac ky tu ngau nhien va ghep thanh mot chuoi captcha
   Tra ve chuoi captcha de hien thi va dung lam gia tri doi chieu, moi lan dang nhap sinh mot ma captcha
   Neu nhap sai thi giu nguyen ma da sinh de nguoi dung nhap lai

5. Kiem tra captcha:
   Ham checkCaptcha(String captchaInput, String captchaGenerate) kiem tra nguoi dung da nhap it nhat mot ky tu
   Dung captchaGenerate.contains(captchaInput) de kiem tra chuoi nhap vao co nam trong ma captcha hay khong
   Cac ky tu nhap vao phai lien tiep va dung thu tu. Ham contains phan biet chu hoa, chu thuong
   Nguoi dung chi can nhap 1 ky tu trong ma captcha (phan biet chu hoa, chu thuong) de hoan thanh captcha
   Neu khong hop le thi tra ve false, ham login hien thi thong bao loi theo ngon ngu da chon va yeu cau nhap lai captcha
   Neu hop le thi tra ve true

6. Dang nhap:
   Ham login(Ebank ebank, Scanner scanner) trong lop Main thuc hien quy trinh dang nhap
   Lan luot doc so tai khoan va mat khau, goi cac ham kiem tra tuong ung cua Ebank
   Neu du lieu khong hop le thi lay thong bao loi theo ngon ngu da chon va yeu cau nhap lai buoc do
   Khi mat khau hop le, sinh va hien thi mot ma captcha, sau do yeu cau nguoi dung nhap captcha
   Neu captcha sai thi thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai voi ma da sinh
   Khi captcha hop le, hien thi thong bao dang nhap thanh cong va ket thuc chuong trinh

* Testcase
- Chon ngon ngu:
1. Nhap bo trong (rong hoac khoang trang): thong bao loi dau vao khong hop le va yeu cau nhap lai
2. Nhap ky tu ("abc") hoac so thuc ("1.5"): thong bao loi khong phai so nguyen va yeu cau nhap lai
3. Nhap lua chon ngoai menu ("0", "4", "-1"): thong bao loi lua chon khong hop le va yeu cau nhap lai
4. Chon "1": loi nhac nhap so tai khoan, mat khau, captcha va cac thong bao loi hien thi bang tieng Viet
5. Chon "2": loi nhac nhap so tai khoan, mat khau, captcha va cac thong bao loi hien thi bang tieng Anh
6. Chon "3": thoat chuong trinh, khong yeu cau nhap thong tin dang nhap

- Nhap so tai khoan:
7. Nhap bo trong (rong hoac khoang trang): thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
8. Nhap chuoi co chu cai, ky tu dac biet hoac khoang trang:
   VD: "12345abcde", "12345@7890", "12345 7890", ket qua: thong bao so tai khoan phai gom dung 10 chu so
9. Nhap it hon hoac nhieu hon 10 chu so:
   VD: "123456789", "12345678901", ket qua: thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
10. Nhap dung 10 chu so:
    VD: "1234567890", ket qua: chap nhan va chuyen sang nhap mat khau
11. Nhap so tai khoan bat dau bang chu so 0:
    VD: "0123456789", ket qua: chap nhan, giu nguyen chu so 0 o dau

- Nhap mat khau:
12. Nhap bo trong (rong hoac khoang trang): thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
13. Nhap mat khau it hon 8 ky tu:
    VD: "Abc1234" (7 ky tu), ket qua: thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
14. Nhap mat khau nhieu hon 31 ky tu:
    VD: 31 chu "a" va mot chu so "1" (32 ky tu), ket qua: thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
15. Nhap mat khau du do dai nhung chi co chu hoac chi co so:
    VD: "abcdefgh", "12345678", ket qua: thong bao loi mat khau phai gom ca chu va so
16. Nhap mat khau co ky tu dac biet hoac khoang trang:
    VD: "Abc@1234", "Abc 1234", ket qua: thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
17. Nhap mat khau dung 8 ky tu va gom ca chu lan so:
    VD: "abc12345", ket qua: chap nhan va chuyen sang buoc captcha
18. Nhap mat khau dung 31 ky tu va gom ca chu lan so:
    VD: 30 chu "a" va mot chu so "1", ket qua: chap nhan

- Kiem tra captcha (gia su ma captcha la: "Ab3D9"):
19. Nhap bo trong (rong hoac khoang trang): thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
20. Nhap mot ky tu co trong captcha:
    VD: "3", ket qua: captcha hop le
21. Nhap nhieu ky tu lien tiep co trong captcha hoac nhap toan bo ma:
    VD: "b3", "Ab3D9", ket qua: captcha hop le
22. Nhap ky tu khong co trong captcha hoac chuoi dai hon ma captcha:
    VD: "Z", "Ab3D90", ket qua: thong bao captcha khong dung va yeu cau nhap lai
23. Nhap cac ky tu co trong ma nhung khong lien tiep hoac sai thu tu:
    VD: "A3", "3b", ket qua: captcha khong hop le
24. Nhap sai chu hoa, chu thuong:
    VD: "ab3D9", ket qua: captcha khong hop le

- Kiem tra luong dang nhap:
25. Nhap so tai khoan sai roi nhap lai dung, mat khau sai roi nhap lai dung, captcha sai roi nhap lai dung:
    Chi chuyen buoc khi du lieu hien tai hop le; hoan tat kiem tra dang nhap sau khi ca ba deu dung
    Thuc hien lai voi ca hai ngon ngu, cac thong bao phai dung ngon ngu da chon
    (Hien thi tieng anh va tieng viet)
26. Thuc hien nhieu lan dang nhap:
    Moi lan deu goi sinh captcha ngau nhien, khong su dung mot ma gan co dinh
    Ma ngau nhien van co the trung nhau, khong ket luan loi chi vi hai lan sinh ra cung mot ma

27. Luong dang nhap bang tieng Viet:
    - Chon 1 (Vietnamese) trong menu
    - Nhap so tai khoan "123": hien thi "So tai khoan phai la 1 so va phai co 10 chu so" va yeu cau nhap lai
    - Nhap so tai khoan "0123456789": chap nhan va dua den buoc tiep theo
    - Nhap mat khau "abc": hien thi "Mat khau phai trong khoang 8-31 ky tu va phai chua ky tu va so" va yeu cau nhap lai
    - Nhap mat khau "abc12345": chap nhan va dua den buoc tiep theo
    - Chuong trinh sinh ma captcha "AkX9g"
    - Nhap "1": hien thi "Captcha sai" va yeu cau nhap lai 
    - Nhap mot ky tu co trong ma captcha do, dung chu hoa hoac chu thuong: "A"
      hien thi "Dang nhap thanh cong" va ket thuc chuong trinh

28. Luong dang nhap bang tieng Anh:
    - Chay lai chuong trinh, chon 2 (English) trong menu
    - Nhap so tai khoan "123": hien thi "Account number must be a number and must have 10 digits" va yeu cau nhap lai
    - Nhap so tai khoan "9876543210": chap nhan va dua den buoc tiep theo
    - Nhap mat khau "abc": hien thi "Password must be between 8 and 31 characters and must be alphanumeric" va yeu cau nhap lai
    - Nhap mat khau "abc12345": chap nhan va dua den buoc tiep theo
    - Chuong trinh sinh ma captcha "Rw0Go"
    - Nhap "1": hien thi "Captcha incorrect" va yeu cau nhap lai 
    - Nhap mot ky tu co trong ma captcha do, dung chu hoa hoac chu thuong: "R"
      hien thi "Login Success" va ket thuc chuong trinh
