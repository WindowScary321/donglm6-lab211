* Mo ta thuat toan
Bubble Sort so sanh tung cap phan tu lien ke tu dau mang den cuoi mang
Neu phan tu ben trai lon hon ben phai thi doi cho hai phan tu. Sau mot luot duyet, phan tu lon nhat trong phan chua sap xep nam o cuoi phan do
- Bat dau voi mang chua sap xep gom n phan tu
- Duyet lan luot cac cap a[j] va a[j + 1]. Neu a[j] > a[j + 1], doi cho chung; neu khong, giu nguyen
(hay rut ngan, lam don gian hoa, ko nen giai thich code)
- Lap lai cac luot duyet cho den khi mang duoc sap xep

* Yeu cau phan mem
1. Nguoi dung nhap so luong phan tu n vao phan mem, phai la so nguyen n > 0
   Neu bo trong, nhap ky tu, nhap so thuc, n = 0 hoac n < 0, thong bao loi va yeu cau nhap lai
   (Nen dua xuong testcase)
3. Tao mang co n phan tu. Moi phan tu la mot so nguyen ngau nhien trong khoang -n den n
4. In toan bo mang truoc khi sap xep
5. Sap xep mang theo thu tu tang dan bang Bubble Sort, sau do in mang sau khi sap xep

* Testcase
1. Nhap rong: thong bao dau vao khong hop le, yeu cau nhap lai
2. Nhap "abc" hoac "5.5": khong phai so nguyen, yeu cau nhap lai
3. Nhap "0" hoac "-5": khong thoa n > 0, yeu cau nhap lai
4. Nhap "1": tao mang mot phan tu; mang sau sap xep giong mang ban dau
5. Nhap "10": tao mang co 10 so, hien ca mang truoc va sau sap xep
   VD: [2, 6, 3, 6, 8, 6, -1, -2, 9, 8], ket qua: [-2, -1, 2, 3, 6, 6, 6, 8, 8, 9]
